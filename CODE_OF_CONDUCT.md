# Community code of conduct

Govee Mac welcomes people of all backgrounds and experience levels.

Be respectful, patient, and constructive. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Help newcomers, credit other people's work, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized content, spam, and sharing private information without consent are not welcome. Maintainers may remove content or restrict participation to protect the community.

Report concerns privately to the repository owner through an available GitHub contact channel. Do not post private details in public issues. Maintainers will handle reports fairly and keep sensitive details private where possible.
